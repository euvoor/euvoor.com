I prefer understanding things to simply making them work.

Some things here are carefully engineered. Others were born at 3 AM because a perfectly reasonable person would have gone to sleep. Both are welcome.

I don’t intend to pretend that every line of code I write is brilliant, every project succeeds, or every problem ends with a heroic victory. Sometimes I win. Sometimes the machine wins. When the machine wins, I take notes.

Revenge is best served with a reproducible test case.
